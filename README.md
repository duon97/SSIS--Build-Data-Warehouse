## Case Study: Credit Card Usage Analysis

**Problem:**  
A bank wanted to analyze credit card usage, monitor card activity, and identify high-risk churn as well as potential customer segments for growth.

**Approach:**  
- Built a **Data Warehouse** and **ETL workflows** to consolidate data.  
- Integrated SQL data into **Power BI dashboards** to visualize usage patterns and segment customers.  
- Conducted **data-driven analysis** to identify churn risk and targetable customer groups.

**Insights:**  
- Detected **16.07% churn risk** among **female customers earning <$40K with Blue cards**.  
- Identified the segment **“Graduate + Married + Male”** as a key group for retention campaigns.

**Actions / Impact:**  
- Targeted **high-risk female customers** with tailored incentives to reduce churn.  
- Promoted **cross-selling and card usage** among at-risk customers.  
- Engaged **“Graduate + Married + Male”** segment to boost conversions by **4.21%**.
